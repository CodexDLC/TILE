Our Philosophy

We write about places,
not listings.

There are already ten thousand ways to find a hotel near the Eiffel Tower. What is harder to find is someone willing to explain why Haussmann demolished medieval Paris in 1853, and what the city lost and gained in the process. That is what we do.

Every entry in our archive begins not with a star rating but with a question: what does this place mean? What happened here, and why should a thoughtful traveller care? We write to readers who find history more interesting than nightlife guides, and who believe that understanding a place is more satisfying than merely visiting it.

Travel is not a reward for working. It is education for living. — Attributed, widely misattributed

Our entries are written by people who have been to these places more than once, who have read the relevant histories, and who prefer precision to enthusiasm. We admit what we do not know. We tell you what to expect, and occasionally what to avoid.
