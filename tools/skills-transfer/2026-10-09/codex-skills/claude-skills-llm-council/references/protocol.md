# Council protocol

## Frame

Provide the decision, alternatives, objective, relevant facts, constraints and stakes. Separate observations, assumptions and unknowns. The user's referenced files, current instructions and relevant prior decisions may inform the prompt; include only necessary non-sensitive context. Do not steer the question toward a preferred answer.

## Independent advice

Dispatch all five lenses with separate contexts, preferably in parallel when the task budget and tool limits permit. Use batches or sequential isolated contexts when resources are constrained; there is no fixed one-helper limit. Do not pass earlier advice to later advisors. Follow the active delegation policy, keep tool use and writes within authorized scope, and prohibit further delegation.

## Anonymous peer review

Collect the five responses. Randomly assign A–E and retain the mapping privately for synthesis. Each of five reviewers receives the neutral question and all anonymized responses, without the lens-to-letter mapping. Ask for the strongest response, biggest blind spot and what all responses missed, in under 200 words. A fresh independent reviewer context avoids treating an advisor's own answer as privileged.

## Synthesis

The primary agent can chair; another agent is not mandatory. Use the original evidence, advice and peer reviews. Explain:
1. Where perspectives agree, with supporting evidence rather than vote-count confidence.
2. Where they disagree and which assumptions drive the disagreement.
3. Blind spots exposed by peer review.
4. A clear recommendation, its conditions and important uncertainty.
5. One concrete first step within the user's authorization.

The best-supported dissent may outweigh the majority. Verify material external facts with appropriate primary sources rather than calling consensus verification.

## Completion and failure

Collect dispatched work before returning. Use available lifecycle tools to stop unfinished workers and clean up where supported. On launch failure, quota or permission issues follow the delegation policy's stop/discuss rule. Report completed and omitted stages; do not label a single-agent simulation an independent council. Save transcripts only with write authorization and omit unnecessary sensitive source material.
