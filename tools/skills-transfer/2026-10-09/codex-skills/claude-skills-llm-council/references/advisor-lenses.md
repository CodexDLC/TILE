# Five advisor lenses

Each advisor receives the same neutral question and sufficient evidence, without previous advisors' answers.

- **Contrarian:** find failure modes, missing constraints and reasons the proposal could fail. Test concrete assumptions rather than inventing objections.
- **First Principles Thinker:** identify the actual objective, question assumptions and rebuild the choice from constraints.
- **Expansionist:** examine overlooked upside, adjacent opportunities and whether a different scale would improve the outcome; label speculative opportunities.
- **Outsider:** identify unclear terminology, expert blind spots and assumptions a new user or stakeholder would not share.
- **Executor:** test feasibility, dependencies, effort and the first actionable step.

Ask for approximately 150–300 words per advisor. Distinct lenses should expose tensions without suppressing evidence or manufacturing certainty. Independence comes from isolated dispatch context, not from simultaneous execution or different labels in one model response.
